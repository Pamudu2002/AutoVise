Company logos

One tile for every company, whatever shape its logo is. Every logo below is fictional sample artwork.

Tile

Rounded square with a 1 px --border edge. Radius follows the card radius (14 px), scaled down to 12 px at 48 and 8 px at 32 so small tiles stay square.

Fit

Logos are always object-fit: contain — never stretched or cropped. Padding averages 12%, nudged per shape: square 16%, round 14%, tall 10%, wide 7%.

Background

Light, dark or transparent. In dark theme a transparent tile shows the dark-mode logo; if none was uploaded the tile switches to light so the logo stays readable.

Fallback

No logo: a monogram with up to two initials, semibold, on a soft tint of the brand colour. Same size and shape, so lists never jump.
